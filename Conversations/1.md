# The Triviality Spectrum: Why Oxygen Exists

## The Fundamental Insight

```yaml
the_triviality_law:
  formula: 'Required Spec Quality = f(Non-Triviality × Edge Potential)'

  principle: |
    Trivial problems with known solutions need minimal specs.
    Non-trivial problems with edge potential need complete specs.

    We're not building Oxygen for Snake games.
    We're building Oxygen for competitive advantages.
```

## Why This Matters: The Edge-Seeking Principle

```yaml

  where_it_lives:

    - Known solutions applied to new markets
    - Unique combinations of existing patterns

    - Proprietary insights about users/markets

  where_it_does_not_live:
```

## Examples Across The Spectrum

```yaml
  spec_needed: |

  edge: 'Zero'

  oxygen_value: 'Negative (overhead for no benefit)'
```
